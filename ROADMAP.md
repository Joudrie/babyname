# Roadmap

## Phase 1: Core feel ✅
Swipe engine (touch, mouse, buttons, arrow keys), undo, dense history list (kept rows green), the box,
boy/girl/unisex colors (blue/pink/lavender) with a neutral theme, two-screen tutorial, deck counter,
saves on the device.

## Phase 2: Decks & categories
- 🌍 category picker: popular origins first (American, English, Irish, Spanish, French, German, Italian),
  then continent → country, each with its name count; several decks on at once.
- Era decks (1800s, 1900s…) and "top names of [year]".
- "Starts with ___" letter filter, combinable with decks.
- Random / Explore deck. A name is sorted once across all decks.
- List mode as an alternative to swiping.

## Phase 3: The box
- Multiple boxes ("Baby #1", "Fiction", "Names I hate").
- Three tiers: love / really like / thinking. Top 50 is a suggestion, not a cap.
- Name variants (Stephen → Steven, Stefan, Esteban, Stepan).
- Origin nudge ("90% of your names are Irish, try the Irish deck").
- Export the box as an image.

## Phase 4: Accounts & partners
- Email / Google login (no promotional email, ever), sync from on-device data.
- Partner pairing by invite code; Matches screen.
- "Email me my top 10".

## Phase 5: Name data (free sources)
- US SSA names 1880–today (popularity, eras, unisex detection).
- Other governments' published lists: England & Wales (ONS), Scotland (NRS), Ireland (CSO),
  France (INSEE), Spain (INE), Italy (ISTAT), Netherlands, Belgium, Nordics, Canadian provinces,
  Australian states.
- Origins and variants from Wikidata. Fiction / literary deck.

## Phase 6: Launch
Domain, hosting, privacy policy, cookie consent, ads (bottom banner + labeled ad card every 50 swipes),
one SEO page per name.

## Ideas parked
Last-name preview on cards, initials check, nicknames, pronounce button, card flip with meaning and a
popularity chart, add to home screen.
